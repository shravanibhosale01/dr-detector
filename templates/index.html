How it works

The image is preprocessed (cropped, resized, contrast-enhanced) and passed through a MobileNetV2-based convolutional neural network fine-tuned on labeled retinal images. The model outputs a severity class from No DR to Proliferative DR, along with a confidence score and risk level.

Understanding Diabetic Retinopathy

Diabetic retinopathy (DR) is an eye condition caused by damage to the blood vessels of the retina due to prolonged high blood sugar. It's a leading cause of vision loss in adults with diabetes.

No DR

Low Risk

No visible retinal damage. Continue routine annual screening.

Mild

Low Risk

Microaneurysms (tiny balloon-like swellings) appear in the retina's blood vessels.

Moderate

Medium Risk

Blood vessels that nourish the retina become blocked, more damage is visible.

Severe

High Risk

Many more blood vessels are blocked, cutting off blood supply to areas of the retina.

Proliferative DR

High Risk

The most advanced stage — abnormal new blood vessels grow, risking severe vision loss.

Prevention Tips

  • Keep blood sugar levels well controlled
  • Manage blood pressure and cholesterol
  • Get annual dilated eye exams
  • Don't smoke
